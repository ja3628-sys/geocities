A sale is just a promise with a deadline. Here's what's working this season:

  • The "Midnight Madness" sale: open late one night only and hand out free coffee. People LOVE feeling like they're in on a secret.
  • Buy one, get one half off: easier on your margins than BOGO free, and it still makes folks grin.
  • The red tag rack: put everything you want gone on ONE rack by the door. Nobody can walk past red.
  • Phone a regular: before the sale opens, call your top 20 customers and give them first pick. They'll remember it!

Promotions don't have to cost a fortune. Try one of these:

Punch cards Ten punches = one freebie. Costs pennies at the print shop and keeps them coming back.
Radio call-in Seventh caller wins a gift certificate. The station gets a contest, you get your name said on the air.
Fishbowl drawing Drop in a business card, win lunch. Hey presto, you just built a mailing list!
Bag stuffers Slip a flyer for next month's sale into every shopping bag. Free advertising that walks out the door.

October's Winner: "The Pumpkin Price Drop"

A hardware store marked down one item every hour the day before Halloween, and wrote the new price on a giant paper pumpkin in the window. Folks stood across the street waiting to see what would drop next! Simple, cheap and FUN. That's a promotion!
