Here's my secret weapon, and it costs about a nickel: CONNECT THE DOTS! Remember those from the back of the cereal box? Kids go nuts for them, grown-ups can't leave one half-finished, and the picture at the end can be ANYTHING you want it to be... like your logo, your storefront, or a great big price tag.

A diner down the road from me started printing dot-to-dots on their paper placemats. The kids stay busy, the parents get to finish their coffee, and guess what? Dessert orders went UP. Here's how to make your own:

  • Keep it between 20 and 40 dots: fewer is too easy, more and the little ones give up before the good part.
  • Make the numbers BIG: crayons are fat and kids are wiggly.
  • Hide your name in the picture: when the last dot connects, they should see YOUR store, not a generic duck.
  • Put your phone number next to dot #1: everybody starts there, so everybody reads it!

Want to turn the dots into dollars? Run a Mystery Sale:

Step 1 Mail out a flyer with a dot-to-dot on it. Don't say what the discount is. Just say "Connect the dots to find out!"
Step 2 When it's finished, the picture spells out the deal: 10% OFF, 25% OFF, or a FREE GIFT. Print a few different versions so nobody knows what the neighbors got!
Step 3 They bring the finished flyer into the store to cash it in. Now you know exactly which mailing brought them through the door.
Step 4 Tape the best-colored ones up by the register. Folks love seeing their kid's artwork on the wall, and they'll bring Grandma by to see it.
